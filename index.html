What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

This visionary scheme aims to provide rooftop solar power systems to one crore households across the country, with several states offering subsidies to facilitate its implementation. For those considering the installation of rooftop solar plants, this initiative presents an excellent opportunity not only for adopting sustainable energy but also for realising significant electricity savings at home.

  1. The scheme aims to equip one crore households with rooftop solar panels to provide electricity from solar energy.
  2. The Government will provide subsidy via direct beneficiary transfer (DBT) for installing a solar rooftop plant at a residential home.
  3. Rooftop photovoltaic panels connect to a building's main power supply, reducing grid electricity consumption and electricity costs.

Subsidy for PM Surya Ghar

  • Subsidy of Rs. 30,000/- for 1 KW system
  • Subsidy of Rs. 60,000/- for 2 KW system
  • Subsidy of Rs. 78,000/- for 3 KW and higher system

Concessional Bank Loans (ROI Starting at 7%)

  • Government banks provide concessional loans for Surya Ghar Yojana, with rates starting at 7%.
  • Loan amount up to Rs. 2 lakh for projects up to 3 KW.
  • Loan amount up to Rs. 6 lakh for projects above 3 KW and up to 10 KW.

Direct Beneficiary Transfer to consumer account

  • As per guidelines, subsidy is transferred to the consumer's account within 30 days of project commissioning by DISCOM.
