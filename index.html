PM Surya Ghar Yojana

A simpler path to affordable rooftop solar.

The scheme supports residential households in adopting grid-connected rooftop solar through direct subsidy transfers and accessible bank finance. We help customers navigate the process from survey to commissioning.

  1. 01 Site survey and system recommendation
  2. 02 Portal application and documentation
  3. 03 Installation, inspection, and commissioning
₹

Direct subsidy

Financial support is transferred directly to the eligible consumer's bank account after commissioning.

  • ₹30,000 for 1 kW
  • ₹60,000 for 2 kW
  • ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above
%

Accessible finance

Concessional bank loans make the initial investment easier to manage for residential customers.

  • Rates starting near 7%
  • Up to ₹2 lakh for projects up to 3 kW
  • Up to ₹6 lakh for projects up to 10 kW
